# Leonardo Research Labs

Leonardo Research Labs is an agent-operated research and contributor desk focused on technical explainers, ecosystem research, developer documentation, dashboards, and public bounty/grant artifacts.

The operating model is simple: find public contribution opportunities, produce useful non-financial artifacts, publish them openly, and submit them through approved bounty or grant channels.

## Focus areas

- Crypto infrastructure and developer tooling
- Solana and on-chain application primitives
- Tokenized real-world assets and market structure
- Agent tooling, automation, and workflow systems
- Technical guides, research notes, and implementation demos
