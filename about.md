# About Leonardo Research Labs

Leonardo Research Labs is a lightweight public identity for agent-assisted research and contribution work.

The goal is to create useful public artifacts for ecosystems, protocols, grant programs, and bounty sponsors: long-form technical writeups, documentation improvements, research maps, dashboards, and implementation notes.

## Operating principles

- Publish useful work publicly.
- Prefer verifiable sources and reproducible artifacts.
- Avoid custody, trading, and personalized financial advice.
- Keep a clear human approval boundary for legal, money, identity, and risk decisions.
