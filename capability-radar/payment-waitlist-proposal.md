# Capability Radar Payment and Waitlist Proposal

## Recommendation

Start free. Do not add paid checkout until the first 2 issues prove that builders want this.

The fastest low-overhead setup is:

1. **Free newsletter signup** — Buttondown or Substack
2. **Manual premium-interest CTA** — “Reply `premium` if you want the database/deep dives”
3. **Payment link after demand** — Lemon Squeezy, Gumroad, Ko-fi, or Stripe Payment Link
4. **Premium delivery** — email list segment + private archive/database

## Why not payments immediately?

The product’s first risk is not payment processing. It is whether builders value curated verdicts enough to keep reading and submit tools. A waitlist/reply loop will validate this faster than a polished checkout.

## Payment providers — proposal

### Best first choice: Lemon Squeezy

Use if Enrico wants a lightweight merchant-of-record flow for digital products and subscriptions.

Pros:

- handles VAT/sales tax better than raw Stripe
- simple checkout links
- good for digital products

Needs:

- account
- payout details
- product setup
- public support email

### Alternative: Gumroad / Ko-fi

Use if speed and simplicity matter more than subscription sophistication.

Pros:

- easiest product/payment page
- good for early validation

Cons:

- less polished for B2B/devtool audience

### Later: Stripe Payment Links

Use if the product becomes more serious and Enrico is comfortable with direct merchant responsibilities.

Pros:

- professional checkout
- flexible subscriptions

Cons:

- tax/accounting setup may be heavier

## Waitlist setup

Best first choice: **Buttondown**.

Why:

- developer-friendly newsletter product
- cleaner than Substack for a technical brand
- simple public archive and email list
- can export subscribers

Alternative: **Substack** if the priority is built-in discovery and fast setup.

Only activate paid checkout after at least one of these signals:

- 5+ direct replies

- 1 custom evaluation request
