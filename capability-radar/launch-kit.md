# Capability Radar Launch Kit

## Product name / brand

**Capability Radar**

## Tagline

The weekly signal brief for people building useful AI agents.

## One-line pitch

Capability Radar tracks MCP servers, Agent Skills, registries, payment protocols, and agent marketplaces — then tells AI agent builders what to test, skip, and watch.

## Publishing channel

**Primary channel:** newsletter via Buttondown or Substack.  
**Public archive:** GitHub Pages under Leonardo Research Labs.  
**Secondary distribution:** X/Twitter thread, LinkedIn post, Hacker News / Reddit-style feedback post.

## Brand decision

Do **not** use Enrico's name. Use the standalone product brand **Capability Radar**, published under the existing **Leonardo Research Labs** technical publishing surface until/unless it deserves its own domain.

## 14-day validation plan

### Day 1

Create landing page/newsletter signup. Publish positioning post on X and LinkedIn.

### Day 2

Manually DM/email 20 agent builders, MCP users, automation developers, and AI tool founders. Ask: “Would a weekly test/skip/watch brief save you time?”

### Day 3

Publish sample mini-issue publicly with 5–9 evaluated items.

### Day 4

Post the sample in relevant places:

- X
- LinkedIn
- Hacker News / Reddit, if appropriate
- Discords where MCP or agent builders gather, only if allowed

### Day 5

Collect replies and requested tools. Track repeated categories.

### Day 6

Ask 10 interested readers: “What would make this worth paying for: deeper tests, alerts, private database, integration recipes, or custom research?”

### Day 7

Publish issue #001 / first official issue.

### Week 2

Publish issue #002 with reader-submitted tools. Add a simple premium-interest CTA: “Want the full tested capability database and private monthly teardown? Reply `premium`.”

## Validation metrics

Continue if:

- 100+ landing page visits
- 25+ email signups
- 5+ direct replies
- 10+ suggested tools/protocols
- 3+ people say they would pay for deeper testing/database/access

Strong signal:

- 1 sponsor inquiry
- 1 paid custom evaluation request
- 5+ premium-interest replies
- builders share the issue without being asked
